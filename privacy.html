Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Fodoolstan application ("App") handles information when an authorized creator connects a TikTok account via TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API.

1. Information Accessed

  • Basic Profile Information (user.info.basic): Open ID, display name, and avatar used solely to verify the connected TikTok destination account.
  • Video Publishing Permissions (video.publish, video.upload): Used solely to upload and publish creator-approved videos from the owner's local workflow.

2. Storage & Security

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally in encrypted credential storage on the owner's workstation and are never sold, rented, or shared with third-party data brokers.

3. Data Deletion & Revocation

You may revoke access at any time from your TikTok account security settings or request deletion of locally stored account records by contacting emarashahn9@gmail.com.
